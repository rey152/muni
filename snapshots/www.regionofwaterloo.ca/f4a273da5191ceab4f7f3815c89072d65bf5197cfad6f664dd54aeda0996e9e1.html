Community Profile

Waterloo Region is a vibrant, healthy community built on the foundations of entrepreneurship, innovation and education.

View the Waterloo Region Community Profile

The Waterloo Region 2023 Community Profile provides a snapshot of our local community. This information can help us to understand the characteristics of our community, and support strategic direction and priorities for our municipality based on what our community needs, today and into the future. 

Themes and topics included in the Community Profile include Waterloo Region population counts and forecast, immigration and migration, building construction, roads and transit, housing, employment, education and more.

Region of Waterloo GeoHub

The Region of Waterloo GeoHub is your go-to place for all things related to geographical information in Waterloo Region. The Region of Waterloo GeoHub offers residents easy access to maps, data, and resources. Explore detailed maps and story maps showing infrastructure, demographics, and the environment. Use interactive tools to make plans, and learn about your community. Use the Region of Waterloo GeoHub to stay informed and contribute to understanding Waterloo Region's geography.

For information on Geographic Information Systems (GIS) services and resources or to request a map or photo call 519-741-4693 or email GIS services through the button below.
