j Year 2025

The Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing welcomes you to the 2025 webpage which houses FIR2025 files as submitted by Municipalities. The Financial Information Return (FIR) is available for viewing/downloading. The deadline for submission of the 2025 FIR is May 31, 2026.

For additional information on the data and this webpage, please refer to the notes at the bottom of the FIR Homepage.

All Municipalities in Ontario are shown in Tables by 5 Geographical Areas.

FIR2025 Files Posted: 318 of the 444 Municipalities in Ontario.
