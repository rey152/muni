## 4) UX outline

The shared card engine renders one card per `call_it_cards` row. Every reveal shows the quoted passage, the source name and date, a link to the record, and an "as of" date.

1. **Intro (once per session).** "These are real past decisions. You're predicting what the record says, not how any person voted." It also shows a base-rate strip: "At the Dec 8, 2025 meeting, 17 motions carried unanimously, 6 carried with some opposition and 1 failed" (F46). *Does not establish:* that other meetings look the same. Show the meeting date on the strip.
2. **Prompt.** Context: date, body (City Council or Regional Council, never merged) and report number. Shows the question and 3–4 options. One option is always a "can't tell / not sure" choice where the honest answer could be that.
3. **Confidence.** Three steps: "Guessing / Fairly sure / Certain". No points, streaks or odds (to avoid a betting frame).
4. **Reveal.** Shows the verbatim outcome with its locator and a "what this does not tell you" box from `does_not_establish`. If the user was certain and wrong, a short line: "A lot of people expect X. Here's why the record says Y." That is the hypercorrection moment. Tallies appear as aggregates only, with no names. Link: "Read the minutes, p.248".
5. **Follow-up (chains only: CI-03, CI-04, CI-06, CI-07).** "What happened next?" with the later record and its date. Where the link between records is interpretation (CI-03), the card says so.
6. **Status-today panel (CI-05, and CI-04 by implication).**
   - *Known:* for example "Repealed June 15, 2026 by By-law 2026-047".
   - *Unknown:* "As of Nov 14, 2026, the records we checked don't show whether an appeal was filed. The notice says the by-law comes into force the day after Nov 8 only if no appeal is filed." Never shown as "in force".
   - *Conditional:* for a card like F50, "Approved in principle, subject to grant funding. Grant outcome: not found."
7. **Source unavailable state.** If a snapshot is `blocked` (as Region FileStream PDFs are), say "The report itself couldn't be retrieved. The decision comes from the minutes page." Never present a missing body as "no report".
8. **Empty state.** If no card passes review for a topic: "No checked past decisions for this topic yet." Never fill it with a made-up example.
9. **End screen.** "You called N of M", plus a list of the misconceptions covered (consent ≠ rubber stamp, passed ≠ in force, City adopts / Province decides, repealed by-laws, a deferral is not a defeat). No leaderboard and no share-your-score feature. Linking to each source is encouraged.

What the feature as a whole does **not** establish: how Council *usually* votes (the cards are a chosen sample), how any member votes, whether a decision was good, or the current state of anything beyond the dated status line.

## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

- **No councillor-level prediction.** City minutes print opponents' names under divided votes (and all names under recorded votes), and Region HTML prints full roll calls (S1, S9, S11, S12). Cards must quote only the aggregate lines ("7 Voting in Favour / 1 Voting in Opposition"). The full record stays one tap away. This applies CAT's "never councillor votes". CI-03's first amendment was proposed by the Mayor (F10). Do not frame it as "the Mayor lost": the card refers to "an amendment proposed at the meeting".
- **Private individuals in source lists.** The Dec 2, 2024 deferral list (F32a) names some landowners who are private individuals. Show the count only.
- **Selection bias / "rigged" framing.** The interesting cards (a failed motion, two-sided votes) are rare: 1 failed motion among 24 dispositions on Dec 8, and none on Jun 15 or Oct 5 (F46–F48). Show the base-rate strip and never imply that failure or dissent is typical.
- **Must not claim:**
  - that By-law 2026-080 (15 King St N) is in force. Its status after Nov 8, 2026 is unknown (F26, F28, F31).
  - that By-law 2025-087 is current (F18).
  - that the 2026-047 PDF is the official text: it is a convenience copy (F20).
  - what the 14 OPA 58 modifications are, or that the public comments caused them. ERO says "no modifications were made" from consultation, and the Notice of Decision gives a provincial-policy reason (F38, F39).
  - that the Region approved OPA 58. Council asked it to, but the Minister decided (F33–F36).
  - that Council adopted OPA 58 for every property in the City without exception. Rec 5 deferred its decision for 47 listed properties (F32a).
  - that the ACIRF holds any money. The Dec 8 text says "consider an initial ACIRF contribution" (F11).
  - that the June 2026 ACIRF policy was the Dec 8 report-back, beyond "same fund name" (F12).
  - that the Region calendar was deferred only once, or directly to Aug 27. It went June 25 → Aug 12 → Aug 27 (F41).
  - what happened to the June 25 amendment to move committee days (F41a).
  - that the Region HTML is the official minutes (F44).
  - the reasons any member voted as they did. No minutes record reasons.
  - "partly carried" for CORP2025-042 without qualification. Every numbered recommendation carried, and only the sub-amendment failed (F8, F10, F13).
- **Draft vs final minutes.** S1, S2, S3, S9 and S10 all carry "READ AND APPROVED" lines with no DRAFT heading. A future card built on a draft (for example Nov 3, 2025, still DRAFT online on 2026-11-13 per PJSON P015, Reported) must be labelled draft.
- **Text-layer artefacts.** P210's text is fragmented ("Bylaw No. 2026 -080", "i s"). Store the raw text verbatim and a separately labelled normalized display string. Keep the source's "filling" [sic].
- **Address label error (F29, resolved).** If a card ever uses the Boehm House by-law, use "15 John Street East" (minutes, notice and file name agree). The heritage page's "15 John Street South" link label is wrong.
- **Staleness.** Status lines must carry an as-of date. Re-check CI-05 when the City updates the heritage page or registers the by-law.
