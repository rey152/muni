> - **What the user does:** picks "Albert Street is closed" and sees work → tender RFT25-01 → budget line → Council meeting, with "record not linked by the City" where the chain breaks.
> - **Why it's distinctive:** works backwards from experience, the way residents actually meet decisions.
> - **Grounding:** P215, P219, P220, P216, P244, P036, P197, P236 (IPPW2025-046), P188.
> - **Tonight:** 2 chains (2 h).
> - **Main risk:** causal claims (a detour may be Region or utility work). Use "recorded connection" wording; a fee chain is the safest. Overlaps N7.
> - Feasibility 3 · Brief fit 4 · Survives scrutiny 3

What this research adds:
- The catalogue's own headline chain, Albert Street, **breaks at the Council hop**. The EngageWR page says the award "is dependent on Council Approval", but no approval record was found.
- The fee chain from the **stormwater charge** is the most complete one: by-law row → Dec 8, 2025 recommendation → enactment list → no-veto instrument → repeal and replacement. *Skeptic pass:* the by-law, the no-veto instrument and a City rates page were re-observed. The two Dec 8 minutes hops could not be re-opened (403). The enactment of 2025-087 rests on the guide (Reported), and the recommendation wording is Unverified this pass.
- It breaks at the first hop back when you start from the **dog licence**.

## 4) UX outline

**Screen 0: "What did you notice?"** Big tappable prompts, grouped by how a resident meets the City:
- On a bill: stormwater, water rate.
- Something I paid for: dog licence.
- A sign or notice: heritage notices.
- At City Hall: speaking time.
- On a map: my ward.
- On my street: Albert St.

The "On my street" group gets a badge reading "may not be City work". A free-text box maps only onto a curated chain id. It never generates a chain.

**Screen 1: The Because card (one chain).** A vertical stack of hops, each a chip with the record name, date, authority badge (City or Region), locator and source link.

Each hop opens to show:
- the verbatim quote, or a research note labelled "summary, not a quote";
- "This shows…" (`establishes`);
- "This does not show…" (`not_establishes`).

The connectors between hops come in three kinds:
- **solid** for `recorded`: the later record cites or quotes the earlier one;
- **dashed**, captioned "matched by title / date, not by an ID", for `reconstructed`;
- **a gap** with the card "Record not linked by the City" or "Not found in Muni's sources", for `not_linked` / `not_found`. Each gap carries a "what we checked" line.

**States**
- **Complete:** stormwater chain. Even here, the top banner says "Recorded connections, not a full explanation of why." The final no-veto hop is dashed.
- **Broken early:** dog licence. The second hop is a gap card: "We couldn't find the Council decision that set this fee in the records we hold. The fee itself is in By-law 2026-047." That is not "no decision exists".
- **Time-sensitive:** heritage 15 King. A status pill reads "Passed Oct 5, 2026 · in force only if not appealed by Nov 8, 2026 · appeal status unknown". There is no green "in force" pill.
- **Source defect:** water rate. The hop shows "The published by-law copy shows '2.' (cut off)" with a page thumbnail, then links to the City rates page ($2.69, Observed) and to the minutes once they are re-captured. It is labelled "the City's rates page shows the 2026 rate; the by-law copy is unreadable here".
- **Not City:** a bus detour (P244) or a Region road (P216). The card stops at hop 1: "This notice doesn't say who ordered the work. Grand River Transit is run by the Region." It does not draw a chain to City Council.
- **Empty:** for a free-text match with no curated chain: "We don't have a traced chain for this yet," followed by links to search and to N26 Follow the Item.
- **Stale:** each source shows "checked 2026-11-14". Albert St shows "This project page's timeline hasn't been updated since 2025."

**Screen 2: Chain sources.** A flat list of every snapshot, with fetched date, url and particular id, for audit.

What the screens do NOT establish:
- causation in the world: that the decision is why you saw the thing;
- that your own bill, licence or address is governed by the row shown;
- current in-force status beyond what the record states;
- the motives or reasons behind a decision.

## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

1. **Causal overreach** (catalogue main risk). Never write "because Council decided X, your street is closed." Write "recorded connection" and "this record lists…". An Albert St closure seen today may be unrelated: the timeline is stale, and Region or utility work is possible (P216, P244).
2. **Do not say "Council approved the Albert St contract."** EngageWR says approval was required, but no approval record was found (F36). Also do not claim the $1,065,260.60 award is the total cost, that work is complete, or that REF 551 funded the tender.
3. **Do not quote a 2026 water or sanitary rate "from the by-law."** The published copy is truncated (F6). Cite the rates page (F7a), or the minutes once re-captured. Attribute the Regional portion to the Region, and do not show the split until F8 is verified. Do not use non-potable rates while F7b is unresolved.
4. **Do not show 15 King St N as "designated / in force."** The appeal outcome is unknown. Likewise, 131 William St W is not designated.
5. **Do not say MD 2025-023 "approved By-law 2025-087."** It approves by class and date and names no numbers.
6. **Dog licence:** do not imply the Dec 8, 2025 meeting set it. The citation list in F10 has no municipal-enforcement fee report, and COM2025-023 has a probable prefix error (F16).
7. **Delegation:** do not state the "Monday 10 a.m. close" as a by-law rule; no s.12 clause says so. Note that Council can change time limits by majority vote (s.12.08).
8. **Ward:** do not name a councillor from the layer (the fields are empty), and do not claim the 2026 label means new boundaries (F27, F28).
9. **Unofficial copies:** P001 and P036 both carry disclaimers (F3, F25), and the card must show them.
10. **Absence is not proof.** Every `not_found` gap must say what was searched.
11. **Unverified minutes hops.** F8–F10, F13 and F15 could not be re-read (events.waterloo.ca 403 to automated access). Re-capture them by hand in a browser before any chip quotes them.
12. **Repo data defects found:** snapshots S-bylaw-2026-047, S-p209 and S-p210 have placeholder urls, and S-dec8-packet points at the 9-page agenda (§3a). Fix these before any chip links out.
13. **Privacy:** the source PDFs and JSON contain staff contacts and supplier addresses. Show none of them.
