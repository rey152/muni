## 1) Spec recap (catalogue, verbatim key lines)

CAT line 187 onward:

> **N113 · Heritage Countdown: Jan 1, 2027.** Listed-but-not-designated heritage properties, and what Ontario law does to them on Jan 1, 2027.
> - **What the user does:** sees status chips ("listed, no notice found", "notice issued", "designated, appeal window", "in force") and recent notices. Examples: the notice of intention for 131 William St W (objections until Nov 6, 2026), and five by-laws passed Oct 5, 2026 (2026-079 to 2026-083, e.g. 15 King St N, OLT appeal until Nov 8, 2026).
> - **Why it's distinctive:** a live legal deadline backed by real Waterloo notices, and it teaches listed vs designated.
> - **Grounding:** P231 (Part IV 42, Part V 137, about 153 listed, a heuristic count per K13), P209, P210, P232, K15. […] Possible extension to 2030 UNVERIFIED: https://www.countylive.ca/deadline-to-protect-heritage-properties-extended/.
> - **Tonight:** a list of about 15 rows, the October notices and one explainer card (2–2.5 h). **Fuller version:** a geocoded map.
> - **Main risk:** the deadline can be changed by regulation, so re-verify today. Say "about" for counts, show no owner details, and don't claim the October batch was driven by the deadline.
> - Feasibility 3 · Brief fit 3 · Survives scrutiny 4

What this pass changes in the spec:
- I found no source that extends the deadline to 2030. That date appears in two places, and neither is current law: as an advocacy request, and as the **old** relisting-ban end date in a stale FAQ on Waterloo's own Engage page (F17). The advocacy sources (Observed in the verification pass): the catalogue's own CountyLive link (https://www.countylive.ca/deadline-to-protect-heritage-properties-extended/, article dated Jun 04, 2024) quotes Architectural Conservancy Ontario's board chair asking "for a deadline extension from Jan. 1, 2025 to Jan. 1, 2030" and says ACO is "pleased that the government has given itself the power to extend the deadline further via regulation." A Toronto councillor's letter to Planning & Housing Committee dated July 14, 2026 (https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2026/ph/bgrd/backgroundfile-289647.pdf, p.1, "RECOMMENDATION" 1) asks the Province "to make a regulation under the Ontario Heritage Act granting a further extension for three years, from January 1, 2027 to January 1, 2030". Both are requests, not law.
- The catalogue warns against claiming the October batch was driven by the deadline. The City's own staff report frames the programme that produced it as deadline-driven (F11). Muni can quote that framing. It still must not say that any single property was designated *because of* the deadline.
- 131 William St W does **not** appear in the July 3, 2025 register's text (F21). It must not be shown as a listed property beating the countdown.

## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

1. **The date may change.** ERO says the dates can be amended by regulation (F5), and Bill 140 amends the OHA (introduced; ERO bulletin dated Oct 28, 2026). I could not read the bill text (403) or the statute (blocked). Never show the countdown without a checked-on date. Re-check e-Laws and ERO before every publish.
2. **Do not cite "s.27(16)" as verified.** Cite ERO 019-8738 and the ontario.ca guidance §3.6.
3. **Do not say listed properties "lose protection and can be demolished" on Jan 1.** The sources say they "must be removed from the register". Listing's protection is the 60-day notice (F10). Removal does not authorise demolition, and later designation is still possible (F9).
4. **Do not give one number for affected properties.** The City sources say 150, 152 and 153 rows, and at least two listed rows are already designated. Do not imply that 153 properties fall off the register.
5. **Do not imply the City will or will not remove any specific property,** or that it has missed the deadline. Staff intend to continue "in 2027" (F15, F20). How that fits with the deadline is not explained in any source I read. Treat it as an open question, not a contradiction to point out.
6. **Do not say any October by-law is in force.** The appeal outcome was not found (F32). "Not found" is not "no appeal".
7. **Do not present 131 William St W as a legacy listed property** (F26), or present its NOID as part of the Bill 23 review. No source I read links them.
8. **Do not attribute the 2030 date to law.** It was an advocacy request, and it was the original relisting-ban end in the stale Engage FAQ (F17).
9. **Privacy.** Notices and reports name staff, the clerk and historical families. Muni needs none of these. Show no owner details. Some P232 profiles name private owners (PJSON P232 parsing_note).
10. **Address normalisation.** "36-40 … even addresses only" vs the register's "38-40 King Street South" (p.17), and "15 John Street East" vs the link text "South". A naive join will mis-chip.
11. **Tone.** State the provincial rule in flat verbs, with no "the Province is erasing heritage" framing (CAT line 184, risk on sibling idea N110: "can read as partisan criticism of the Province").
