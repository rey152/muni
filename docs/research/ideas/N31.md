## 1) Spec recap (catalogue, verbatim)

Source: `data/inventory/Muni-new-ideas-catalogue.md`, lines 499–505.

> **N31 · Five Minutes at the Mic.** Rehearse a Council delegation with the correct time limit.
> - **What the user does:** picks an item. The app applies P202 (10 or 5 minutes), offers a structure (who I am, which item, what I'm asking, my evidence) and a countdown timer. Rehearsal stays on the device.
> - **Why it's distinctive:** moves from knowing about participation to practising it.
> - **Grounding:** P202, P001 ss.12.0 and 25.0, P203. Boal: https://arte-util.org/projects/theatre-of-the-oppressed-legislative-theatre/.
> - **Tonight:** 1.5–2 h.
> - **Main risk:** P202 cites no by-law, so reconcile it with P001. Never simulate councillors. Simplest form: a static "how to delegate" card with a timer.
> - Feasibility 4 · Brief fit 3 · Survives scrutiny 3

### 2c) City drift: P202 compared with P001

The guide's open question (l.3820) gets a clause-level answer here. **Status: Observed (my comparison of the two texts above).**

| # | Topic | P202 says | P001 says | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 10/5 split | "by Friday at 10 a.m." → "you get 10 minutes" | "before 10:00 a.m. on Friday preceding the meeting" → "a maximum of ten minutes", otherwise "a maximum of five minutes" | **Agrees on values.** Two differences in wording: "by" vs "before" leaves registration at exactly 10:00 ambiguous, and "you get" vs "a maximum of". The timer should say "up to". |
| D2 | Registration close | "registration closes at 10 a.m. on the Monday of the meeting" | **Silent.** s.12.0 has no closing time. "Monday" appears in P001 only at 2.01 (inaugural meeting, "first Monday of the Council term" / "next following Monday") and 11.03 (minutes "approved by the fourth Monday following the meeting"). Neither is about delegations. | **Unsourced in the by-law.** It may be administrative practice under the Clerk; this is Unverified. Show it as "City guidance page says". It also assumes a Monday meeting, which fits "Council meets most Mondays" (Observed, https://www.waterloo.ca/council-and-committees/council-and-committee-meetings/how-council-meetings-work/). The rule for a meeting on another day is not stated. |
| D3 | What you may speak to | "an item on the public agenda" | 12.05 limits agenda delegations to staff reports, public meetings and notice-of-motion items; 12.13–12.16 set a separate non-agenda route | P202 is narrower in one way (it omits the non-agenda route) and broader in another (it says any agenda item, where 12.05 says only three item types). |
| D4 | Exception | "except when a public discussion period is already part of the meeting" | 12.03 + 25.07: Planning Act–type public meetings need no advance registration, and unregistered speakers get "five minutes" | Consistent, but P202 does not state the 5-minute figure for that case. |
| D5 | Time is not fixed | "stay within your allotted time" | 12.06 (motion to continue), 12.08 (majority vote may limit or extend) | P202 omits that the time can change. |
| D6 | Repeat bar | not mentioned | 12.15 iv: non-agenda matters only; 6 months since a Council decision or 3 months since the same delegation attended | No conflict, but the City has a repeat bar (narrower than the Region's) that P202 does not mention. |
| D7 | Official status | no citation, no date | consolidation "not an exact reproduction … for information purposes only" | Neither page is the official signed text. The timer's values rest on an unofficial consolidation that agrees with the guidance page on D1. |

**Verdict on the City:** the 10 and 5 values that the timer needs are supported by both sources, quoted verbatim (D1). The Monday 10 a.m. close rests only on P202 (D2).

## 4) UX outline

The plan does not save rehearsal text. The catalogue requires that rehearsal stays on the device, so a draft is kept only in the viewer's browser storage, if at all, and the page works without it. Muni never simulates councillors.

**Screen 1: Choose where you'd speak.** Choices: "City of Waterloo Council" and "Region of Waterloo Council or committee". Each card shows its source line, for example "Rules from Procedure By-law 2019-057 s.12.04 (unofficial consolidation, last passed Oct 5, 2020)" and "Rules from Region By-law 26-017 s.10.1, in force Apr 22, 2026".
- *Region unknown state:* a permanent banner: "The Region scheduled a replacement procedural by-law for Oct 14, 2026. We could not check whether it passed (checked Nov 14, 2026). Confirm limits with the Regional Clerk."
- *Does NOT establish:* that any meeting is scheduled, or that you are eligible to speak on your topic.

**Screen 2: Your time.** The user picks the condition. Muni does not infer it.
- City options: "I registered before 10:00 a.m. on the Friday before the meeting" → up to 10:00. "I registered later" → up to 5:00. "Public meeting, not registered" → 5:00 (25.07). "Not sure" → defaults to the **shorter** limit, with the note "If you're not sure, practise for 5 minutes."
- Region options: "Registered at least 24 hours before" → 5:00. "Registered later" → up to 3:00. "New addendum item, registered ≥1 hour before" → 5:00. "Not sure" → 3:00.
- Each option shows the verbatim clause on tap. The City card also shows P202's wording, with a small note: "The City's guidance page also says registration closes at 10 a.m. Monday. We didn't find that in the by-law text."
- Always shown: "This is the maximum the rules state. The Chair keeps time and Council may shorten or extend it (12.06, 12.08)" or, for the Region, "Extensions are by permission of the Chair (10.1.4)".
- *Empty state:* if a `DelegationLimit` row lacks a `rule_passage`, the timer is disabled and shows "No stated limit found." No default number appears.
- *Does NOT establish:* that you are registered, or which tier the Clerk will give you. Registration needs Clerk confirmation in the Region (10.1.8).

**Screen 3: Outline (optional).** Four prompts, each tied to a source:
1. "Who I am" (individual, organization or representing someone): P001 12.02 iv–vi and the City form's "Are you addressing Council as".
2. "Which item": P001 12.02 ii. If the user picks a Muni matter, show its agenda label only where a record establishes it.
3. "What I'm asking Council to do": P001 12.02 iii; Region guide p.3 "Be clear about what you are asking Council to do".
4. "My evidence".

The text is never sent anywhere. A caution appears: "If you send this as written comments, the City says they become part of the public record; the Region says this includes any contact information." This links to N41 (Exposure Preview).
- *Does NOT establish:* that the item is open for delegations. City 12.05 limits agenda delegations to staff reports, public meetings and notice-of-motion items, and general participation guidance does not prove an opportunity exists for a specific matter.

**Screen 4: Rehearse.** A countdown set to the chosen limit, with Start, Pause and Reset. A calm visual and an optional 1-minute marker, without urgency styling. The Region marker can cite "The Chair typically warns delegations when they are nearing the end of their time limit" (guide p.3). The marker itself is Muni's choice, and the screen says so. At 0:00: "Time. At a real meeting the Chair would ask you to stop (12.06)" or, for the Region, "Time limits are strictly followed (Region guide p.3)". Stopping early shows the Region guide's "It’s always okay to stop speaking before your time is up if you’ve made your point."
- *After the timer:* "Councillors may ask questions; you are not obliged to answer (City 12.09)." No simulated questions.
- *Does NOT establish:* how a real meeting will run, or that speaking influences the decision.

**Screen 5: Before you go.** Static checklist with quotes:
- City materials: "upload or email them before the meeting" (no deadline stated; 12.12 leaves it to the Clerk).
- Region materials: by-law "9:00 a.m. the business day before" vs page "24 hours … 12 slides maximum", shown side by side as a flagged difference.
- Repeat bars: City 12.15 iv (non-agenda matters) and Region 10.1.5 (6 months).
- Registration links: the official forms only.

## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

1. **Do not call P202's Monday 10 a.m. close a by-law rule.** It appears only on the guidance page (DX-city-monday-close).
2. **Do not cite By-law 22-051** for Region rules. Do not present 26-017 as current without the Oct 14, 2026 caveat. Re-check the Region by-laws page on every build, and treat a new by-law number as a breaking change that disables the Region timer until it is reviewed.
3. **Do not present "12 slides" or "24 hours for materials" as Region by-law rules.** They come from the page and conflict with 26-017 s.10.1.7 (DX-region-materials).
4. **Do not say "you get 10 minutes".** The clauses say "a maximum of", and Council or the Chair can change the time (12.06, 12.08; 10.1.4).
5. **Do not compute the user's tier from a meeting date.** The implementation plan says "A date is never derived from a meeting date". City meetings are "most Mondays", and the Friday/Monday rule is undefined for other days. The user self-selects the tier.
6. **Do not mix up the Region's 10-minute "presentations" (s.11) with delegations.**
7. **Do not imply City committees use the same limits.** P202 covers council meetings only. P001's long title covers committees, but no committee-specific delegation limits were checked. City committees: Unverified.
8. **Do not say the City consolidation is the legal text.** Show its caveat (PA-p001-caveat).
9. **No simulated councillors and no predicted questions.** Do not suggest that a delegation changes outcomes ("Public input received is not proof that it influenced a decision", evidence skill).
10. **Privacy:** rehearsal text stays on the device. Never upload it. Warn that real submissions become public.
11. **Electronic meetings:** at the Clerk's discretion, an Electronic Meeting allows public delegations only "by way of electronic submission received in advance of the meeting … or by electronic participation in the electronic meeting on items already on the agenda" (32A.05). The timer must not imply an in-person slot always exists.
12. **Unverified items that must not appear as facts:** amendments to City 2019-057 after Oct 5, 2020; the outcome of the Region's Oct 14, 2026 replacement; why P202's Monday close exists.

## 6) Verdict: **GROUNDED**, with two hard conditions

The core of the feature is a countdown set to a stated limit, plus an outline. It is buildable honestly:
- **City 10 and 5 minutes:** verbatim in P001 12.04, and P202 agrees on the values. A public meeting without registration gets 5 (25.07). Observed.
- **Region 5 and 3 minutes:** verbatim in By-law 26-017 s.10.1.2–10.1.3, and P203 agrees on the values. Observed.
- **Rehearsal itself is official advice:** the Region guide says "Never give a presentation without practicing at least once to be sure that it will fit into the time you are allowed" (p.5). Observed.
- **The outline structure** comes from P001 12.02 and the City delegation form rather than Muni's own design.

Conditions:
1. The Region timer must carry the "replacement scheduled Oct 14, 2026, outcome unverified" banner. Better, a person checks the Region's Oct 14 minutes before launch (eScribe blocked automated access).
2. The deadlines found only in guidance (City Monday close; Region noon one week before, 24-hour materials, 12 slides) are shown as "guidance page says" with the discrepancy flagged, and are never fed into the timer.

This dossier also closes the guide's open item at l.3820 for P202: on the time limits P202 and P001 agree, the Monday close is not in the by-law, and the scope wording differs. The Drift Sentinel rule pairs above can reuse that finding. No backup idea is needed. If the Region condition cannot be met in time, ship the City-only timer and show a Region card with quotes and no timer. That also matches the catalogue's "simplest form: a static how-to-delegate card with a timer".
