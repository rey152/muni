Every row shows the verbatim date phrase, how precise it is ("exact date", "month", "season", "year only"), the source and the checked date. ## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

1. **Transition law (highest risk).** Do not claim as fact any of: whether delegations survive a new term; whether Waterloo was in a s.275 restricted period, or whether O. Reg. 530/22 s.11 was triggered for its Mayor; what s.7(1.1) means for the 2027 budget's dates. The text of s.7 and s.11 is now Observed (skeptic pass, e-Laws Word download, consolidation from June 22, 2026), but applying it to Waterloo is interpretation. The catalogue's note that "AMCTO and Cochrane disagree" was not re-checked. AMCTO's summary matches the regulation text. Quote the regulation verbatim with "as of {date}". Municipal Act s.275 and Part VI.1 are still unread, so label anything from them "summary from {source}".
2. **Staff timelines are not Council decisions.** CORP2026-031 recommendations 2–3 are proposals. Their adoption is unread (403). The budget page and the June summary state schedules, not decisions.
3. **"Inherits" must not read as blame.** Use "scheduled for 2027", "in progress" and "next step", never "left undone" or "failed to finish" (catalogue: "Say 'in progress', not 'left open'"). The Urban Forest slip from June 2026 to Feb 2027 gets a neutral "previously" note with no cause.
4. **No people.** No winners, no councillor or candidate names, no ward-by-ward "what your councillor left". Ruling 15 applies, and the P224 ward layer has empty councillor fields. The results certificate exists but was not opened.
5. **Heritage legal effect.** Do not say "152 properties will lose protection on January 1, 2027". The page's "currently" is undated, designations happened in 2026, and the NOID-pending rule is not read. Say "The City says listed properties not designated by January 1, 2027 must be removed from the Register."
6. **Absence claims.** "No newer mayoral direction" and "no designation notice for 131 William" are index observations on a check date (SKILL lines 502–503). They need that wording.
7. **Undated phrases.** "by November 30" (Park survey) has no year, and "spring/summer 2027" and "mid-2027" are seasons. Keep them as printed and never convert them to fake exact dates.
8. **Date-sensitivity.** The feature goes stale fast: committee applications close Nov 17, the Park survey Nov 30, the budget releases Dec 14. Compute states in the browser from the local date, as Windows Wall does (PLAN line 200).
9. **Calendar arithmetic is not a record.** "Monday Nov 16" is derived from by-law s.2.01 and the calendar. It must be labelled "the by-law's rule would put it on…", or omitted.
10. **Region versus City.** The Regional Chair appointment (F10) is the Region's. Do not mix it into "the new Council".
