## 4) UX outline

**Screen A: Case board.** It shows 3–5 cards: "Case RD-01: Two pages, one deadline", and so on. Each card shows its authority (City or Region), its "checked on" date and its difficulty.
- *Empty state:* "No open cases right now. Every case we had has been re-checked or resolved." Links to the archive.
- *Does NOT establish:* that the City's records are generally unreliable. The board always mixes in at least one control case.

**Screen B: Case view.** Two panes show source A and source B. Each pane has its verbatim quote, a link that opens the official page, the locator and the fetch date. The resident's prompt is "What differs?" and they choose from: the unit, the date, the label meaning, a different file, or nothing.
- *Unknown state:* if a source could not be re-fetched today, the pane reads "Could not re-check this source on <date>. Showing the text as captured on <date>." The case is labelled "unverified today".
- *Does NOT establish:* which source is correct.

**Screen C: Reveal.** It has three parts:
1. **What differs:** the field and the two values.
2. **How you could spot it yourself:** for example, open the by-law PDF and search "business days", or compare the file numbers in the map popup.
3. **What this does not tell you:** for example, "A difference does not show which source is right; for the deadline, rely on By-law 24-043 s.8.2, and the Region's registrar can confirm."

Control cases reveal "These are two different applications (Z-25-11 and Z-26-08). Not a conflict."
- *Does NOT establish:* a decision, an outcome or fault.

**Screen D: Flag a new one (merged with N91 Dispute This).** It asks for source A's url and quote, source B's url and quote, and "what looks different". The flag goes to a local queue (staff-only), with routes for "Muni misread", "records disagree" and "a record looks wrong". The resident sees "Received. Not yet checked."
- *Error state:* a flag must have two urls.
- *Does NOT establish:* that the flag is correct. Flags never appear publicly as cases until a reviewer confirms them.

**Screen E: Archive.** It lists resolved or stale cases with their resolution text, for example "The Region updated its page on <date>". Nothing is shown as resolved unless a fresh capture shows the change.

## 5) Risks and what must not be claimed

**Never claim:**
- Never say which source is right, beyond pointing to the instrument as the binding text. Say "the by-law says", not "the page is wrong".
- Never say the City made an error. RD-05 is the research's "probable prefix error" and has not been confirmed by the City.
- Never treat a layer status ("Application Approved"), "Closed", or a collection's membership as a decision. Only minutes, a by-law or a tribunal decision establishes one.
- Never present P225's CREATE_DATE/UPDATE_DATE as the date of any event.
- Never call F21 a contradiction. It compares 2022 and 2025.
- Never run Dec 8 / Sept 22 cases (RD-05, RD-06, RD-07, F19) as verified until a human has read them in a browser. Automated access to events.waterloo.ca returned 403 on 2026-11-14.

**Risks:**
- *Trust erosion and embarrassment.* Cases are framed as "reading skills", not as "gotchas". Each case is paired with a control case. Wording stays neutral and never attributes fault. Staff are notified before a case goes live (catalogue main risk).
- *Staleness.* Layers change: the Online Viewer layer was last edited 2026-11-09 and the MAINT layer 2026-07-06. Each case stores `found_on` and is re-fetched before display, and a case that no longer reproduces moves to the archive.
- *Personal details.* Leave out P250 (named officials' declarations) and any case built on correspondence or Q&A usernames (P205). Development site addresses are public application sites, but show file numbers alongside them, not owners.
- *Region vs City confusion.* P253 is a Region instrument. Label it as Region.
- *Join method.* Slug joins are approximate. F5 and F9 come from my script, not from a City crosswalk. Show the join as "matched by link".
